THEORY-FIRST CHARACTER-WORLD ENGINE · v0.1

Characters first. World second.

Most systems build a finished world and place characters inside it. This one reverses the relationship: source-grounded characters get only their local situation; the world gets only enough bare substrate for action to begin, and it resists. Watch what their continued attempts to act force the world to become. The theory of this program is in halfworld-theory.md.
