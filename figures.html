INSTRUMENT

The Figure as a Latent Program

A recorded person is treated as a generative program: transcript turns are sparse samples of its output. This instrument reconstructs the program's low-dimensional parameters — the Centaur Box's eleven psychological axes — from transcript evidence only, and draws the posterior as a radar with per-axis confidence. Every score is traceable to the exact turns that produced it; where the record is silent, the radar stays at its prior and says so.

THEORY — HOW THE LATENT PROGRAM IS MODELED

The claim. A figure in FIGURE CORPS is not a bundle of biographical facts; it is a latent program — a generative process that, given a conversational situation, produces this person's kind of utterance. The transcript is a sparse, biased sample of that program's outputs. The Centaur Box already models agents with an eleven-dimensional disposition vector θ = (openness … decisiveness). Integration: treat θ as the low-dimensional parameter surface of the figure's latent program. The radar is not decoration — it is the posterior over θ given the record.

Inference (Tenenbaum PLoT, made honest). The prior is maximum-entropy: every axis at 3, confidence 0 — the instrument claims nothing before evidence. Each target-speaker turn is scanned for observable markers; each marker is an evidence event e = (turn, axis, direction, weight, quote). A turn's events yield an observation v = 3 + 1.5·tanh(Σ±w), and the posterior mean updates with a decreasing gain θ ← θ + (v−θ)/(n+2) — a fixed-form conjugate update, so early turns move the estimate freely and later turns refine it. Per-axis confidence c = n/(n+4) sets the band width: few observations, wide band; the radar literally shows how much the record has earned the right to say.

Two channels — the centaur. Testimony and behavior are different evidence. The told channel scores first-person claims (“I decided right then”, “I was so scared”) — the story the figure narrates about itself. The shown channel scores manner — hedges, causal connectives, imperatives, assent, exclamation, expansiveness — the program's texture regardless of what it claims. Where the polygons diverge past threshold, that is the myth gap (Barthes): the figure's self-mythology measured against its own tape. Neither channel is “the truth”; the pair is the figure.

Evidence discipline (FIGURE CORPS contract). No axis moves without a citable turn; clicking any axis lists the exact quotes and directions that produced its score. Silence is preserved as prior + wide band, never filled with invented biography. The optional LLM modeler must return per-axis turn citations and is drawn as a separate polygon — it can disagree with the lexical estimator in public, but it cannot overwrite it.

Closing the loop (Centaur Box). Exporting hands the posterior over as a Centaur Box Belief. Loaded as an agent's self-model, the figure's latent program becomes the hidden ground truth of a Centaur Box encounter — and the Box's Observer, updating its own radar from conversation alone, is now running the same inference this instrument ran on the archive. Comparing the Observer's converged model with this instrument's transcript posterior is the held-out test at the level of character rather than sentences: did the performance carry the program, or only its lines?
